Boxing

Ring Announcing

From standup comedy stages to the bright lights of the boxing ring — Joe Nipote brings his showman's presence and big voice to fight night.

Joe has expanded his entertainment career into ring announcing, introducing countless professional bouts live on HBO and Showtime.

"The same showman instincts that built a standup career — command of a room, timing, presence — translate perfectly to the ring. Joe brings something special to fight night."

Notable Events

  • Announced Shawn Estrada's professional debut at Citizen's Business Bank Arena, Ontario CA — HBO broadcast
  • WBC CABOFE Caribbean Heavyweight Championship at Tachi Palace Hotel & Casino
  • Multiple televised bouts on HBO and Showtime
  • Exclusive announcer for Goosen/Tutor Promotions
