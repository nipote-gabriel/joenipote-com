About Joe

Joe started as a standup at The Comedy Store during the heyday of comedy in the late 70s. After years of touring and headlining clubs across the country he launched a career in film and TV, landing a leading role on the hit NBC series, VIPER. Joe has since played memorable roles in many major films, working with directors like Steven Spielberg… and you've probably seen him guest star in your favorite sitcom.
