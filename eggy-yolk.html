Eggy Yolk™

Children's author, storyteller, and school performer.

Eggy Yolk and the Dirty Rotten Dozen™

Joe Nipote is an actor and comedian, best known to children as the voice of Uncle Stretch from the film Casper, and as Uncle Marco from Suite Life on Deck.

Eggy Yolk and the Dirty Rotten Dozen is Joe's first children's book — and like Sheriff Eggy, he loves to crack jokes and make people laugh. Unlike Eggy, Joe eats eggs every day.

School & Community Appearances

Joe Nipote is available for public appearances as a children's storyteller for your school, bookstore, charity organization, or church group. He performs for elementary students in grades 2–6 and delivers an experience kids won't forget.

What Joe Brings

  • Live reading of Eggy Yolk and the Dirty Rotten Dozen™
  • Video clips from his TV and film career
  • Stand-up comedy adapted for kids
  • Tongue twisters and clean jokes
  • Q&A session emphasizing the importance of writing

Who It's For

  • Elementary schools (grades 2–6)
  • Bookstores and libraries
  • Charity organizations
  • Church groups and community events
  • Private and corporate family events

Bring Joe's Eggy Yolk experience to your school or organization. Reach out directly to check availability and discuss the details.
